ChargeGhar privacy

This is the prototype version of ChargeGhar. It keeps your city, bookings, reviews and listing only on your own device, in the browser's local storage. It does not send them to any server and does not use analytics or ads.

If you turn on Google Maps, Google receives the map requests the page makes, under Google's own privacy policy.

Update this page before launch. A real version will collect sign-in details, location and payment information, and this page must say exactly what is collected, why, and how to delete it.
